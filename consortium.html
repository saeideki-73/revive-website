The consortium

Consortium

REVIVE brings together eight beneficiary institutions and ten associate partners across nine countries — universities and research institutes, technology companies, infrastructure operators and international standards bodies — covering the full value chain from sensing and digitalisation to certification and field deployment.

United Kingdom Netherlands Germany Italy Slovenia Hungary Sweden Switzerland France
Coordinator
University of Southampton
Coordinator United Kingdom

Coordinates REVIVE on behalf of the consortium, leads predictive digital-twin modelling (WP3) and management (WP8), and co-leads integration into infrastructure asset management (WP5) — drawing on large-scale structural testing laboratories and probabilistic modelling platforms.

Academic Partners
TU Delft
Netherlands

Leads WP1, advancing multi-sensor NDE and SHM for condition evaluation, with multi-modal laboratories spanning ultrasonic arrays, ground-penetrating radar, infrared thermography and corrosion monitoring.

Helmut Schmidt University
Germany

Leads WP2, building REVIVE's digital backbone through AI-driven data fusion and digital-twin integration, supported by high-performance computing and AR/VR visualisation labs.

University of Roma Tor Vergata
Italy

Co-leads WP4 with the University of Bristol, developing and validating low-carbon recycled-aggregate concretes and hybrid retrofitting systems.

University of Bristol
United Kingdom

Co-leads WP4 and leads WP7, combining FRP fabrication and retrofitting facilities with the project's outreach and dissemination strategy.

University of Ljubljana
Slovenia

Supports WP5 with reliability-based safety assessment and structural monitoring, translating academic advances into maintenance protocols for infrastructure owners.

Research Institutes
HUN-REN SZTAKI
Hungary

Institute for Computer Science and Control — contributes to WP2 with surrogate modelling, explainable AI and uncertainty quantification, strengthening the trustworthiness of REVIVE's predictive models.

ZAG
Slovenia

Slovenian National Building and Civil Engineering Institute — co-leads WP5 and leads WP6, running accredited durability testing and field demonstration campaigns aligned with Eurocode and EN standardisation.

Industry Partners
SHM Next
Netherlands

Structural health monitoring technology company contributing sensing and data-platform expertise to WP5's asset-management framework.

SVTI
Switzerland

Technical inspection association supporting materials validation and asset-management integration in WP4 and WP5.

Timezyx
Sweden

Contributes modelling and validation expertise to WP5's asset-management framework.

Atkins Réalis
United Kingdom

Global engineering consultancy supporting materials validation, asset management and field demonstration across WP4, WP5 and WP6.

Infrastructure & Standards Partners
National Highways
United Kingdom

Asset owner providing operational bridges and tunnels for REVIVE's field demonstration and industry deployment activities.

Network Rail
United Kingdom

Asset owner supporting field validation of REVIVE's inspection and monitoring solutions on live rail infrastructure.

Rijkswaterstaat
Netherlands

The Dutch Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management, providing access to national infrastructure for demonstration and validation.

Die Autobahn
Germany

German federal motorway operator supporting field trials of REVIVE's condition-monitoring solutions.

fib
Switzerland

International Federation for Structural Concrete — aligns REVIVE's assessment protocols with the fib Model Code and Eurocode 2.

RILEM
France

International scientific association supporting standardisation and dissemination of REVIVE's testing and materials methods.
