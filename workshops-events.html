Upcoming workshop 23 September · Hartley Suite, University of Southampton

REVIVE Technical Workshop

Bringing together researchers and industry to advance assessment, monitoring, and service life extension of infrastructure.

Register on Eventbrite
Read more

Organised by Dr. Mohammad Mehdi Kashani as part of the Horizon Europe MSCA-funded REVIVE project, this day-long technical workshop bridges the gap between academic research and practical infrastructure management. The event features presentations from consortium partners, infrastructure owners, and industry representatives, focusing on the latest developments in vulnerable reinforced concrete infrastructure.

Key topics and discussions include
  • Non-Destructive Evaluation (NDE) and Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)
  • Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, and Digital Twins
  • Infrastructure Asset Management and Service Life Prediction

Non-Destructive Evaluation and Service Life Extension of Vulnerable Reinforced Concrete Infrastructure.
